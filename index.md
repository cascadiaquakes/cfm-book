# CRESCENT Community Fault Model

The [Cascadia Region Earthquake Science Center (CRESCENT)](https://cascadiaquakes.org) Community Fault Model (CFM) is a three-dimensional representation of faults across the Cascadia Subduction Zone and surrounding region. Explore it in the [CFM Viewer](https://cfm.cascadiaquakes.org) in 2D and 3D.

:::{note}
CFM v1.0 is currently under review and not yet published.
:::
